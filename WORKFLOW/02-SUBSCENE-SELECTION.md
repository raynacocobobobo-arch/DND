# 02 — Subscene Selection

## Purpose
Choose one representative drawable internal location.

## Inputs
- location research; candidate internal areas

## Required work
- Use the approved spec and MASTER documents.
- Preserve evidence labels: `【SRD-5.1】`, `【SRD-5.2.1】`, `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`, `【OFFICIAL-INDEX】`, `【PROJECT】`.
- Record unresolved facts instead of filling them with generic fantasy assumptions.

## Output
- `RESEARCH/02-SUBSCENE-DECISION.md`

## Gate relationship
- **Gate 1**
- No downstream stage starts before its required gate is approved.

## Pass criteria
- one subscene survives a scored comparison.

## Failure return
- Stage 02.

## Do not
- combining multiple districts into one image.
