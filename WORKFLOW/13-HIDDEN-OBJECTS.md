# 13 — Hidden-Object Design

## Purpose
Select fair, diegetic targets and hiding logic after the scene is visually approved.

## Inputs
- Gate 5 scene

## Required work
- Use the approved spec and MASTER documents.
- Preserve evidence labels: `【SRD-5.1】`, `【SRD-5.2.1】`, `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`, `【OFFICIAL-INDEX】`, `【PROJECT】`.
- Record unresolved facts instead of filling them with generic fantasy assumptions.

## Output
- `target design draft`

## Gate relationship
- **Gate 6**
- No downstream stage starts before its required gate is approved.

## Pass criteria
- varied target types; intentional difficulty; no microscopic hiding.

## Failure return
- Stage 13.

## Do not
- blur-based hiding; targets that force repainting without need.
