# 08 — Props and Creatures

## Purpose
Lock functional props, tools, creatures and purposeful magic.

## Inputs
- location research; cast; SRD equipment/tools; official creatures

## Required work
- Use the approved spec and MASTER documents.
- Preserve evidence labels: `【SRD-5.1】`, `【SRD-5.2.1】`, `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`, `【OFFICIAL-INDEX】`, `【PROJECT】`.
- Record unresolved facts instead of filling them with generic fantasy assumptions.

## Output
- `PRODUCTION/08-PROPS-CREATURES.md`

## Gate relationship
- **Gate 3**
- No downstream stage starts before its required gate is approved.

## Pass criteria
- props and creatures are sourced or labeled; magic has a task.

## Failure return
- Stage 08.

## Do not
- decorative glow-orbs; monsters added only for spectacle.
