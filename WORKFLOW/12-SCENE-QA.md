# 12 — Scene QA

## Purpose
Correct visual faults before game mechanics are added.

## Inputs
- integrated scene

## Required work
- Use the approved spec and MASTER documents.
- Preserve evidence labels: `【SRD-5.1】`, `【SRD-5.2.1】`, `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`, `【OFFICIAL-INDEX】`, `【PROJECT】`.
- Record unresolved facts instead of filling them with generic fantasy assumptions.

## Output
- `PRODUCTION/scene/ corrected final candidate`

## Gate relationship
- **Gate 5**
- No downstream stage starts before its required gate is approved.

## Pass criteria
- proportion, species, face, action, density, composition, background and color pass.

## Failure return
- Stage 12 or smallest causal stage.

## Do not
- new methodology for local faults; hiding targets before visual pass.
