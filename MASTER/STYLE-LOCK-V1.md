# D&D 5E Hidden-Object Global Style Lock V1

## Core statement

> **A character comic that contains a setting, not a big setting containing many characters.**

Characters are the primary read. Architecture and landscape establish place but never reduce the cast to decorative dots.

## Illustration language

- clean European fantasy adventure ensemble comic;
- stable black hand-drawn line art;
- flat colors with restrained cel shading;
- bright but controlled saturation;
- warm/lively overall readability;
- no photorealism;
- no painterly rendering;
- no 3D-render look;
- no cinematic depth-of-field blur.

## Character proportions

- approximately **5–5.5 heads tall**;
- head may be slightly enlarged for readability, never chibi;
- natural limbs and functional hands;
- faces remain distinct at final viewing scale;
- species silhouette differences must survive crowd density.

## Crowd scale compression

- foreground: **105–110%**;
- core midground: **100%**;
- rear/high platform: **85–90%**.

Avoid realistic continuous distance shrinkage that makes rear characters unreadable.

## Composition

- weak/compressed perspective;
- stage-like horizontal spread;
- event islands rather than uniform scattering;
- 8–12 readable interaction groups;
- foreground figures do not become giant cut-off portraits;
- rear figures still show faces, silhouettes and actions.

## Character behavior

Characters act rather than pose. Prefer:
- working;
- carrying;
- checking;
- arguing;
- repairing;
- trading;
- helping;
- observing;
- performing;
- hiding/stealing;
- ritual/administrative behavior.

## Background

Background is one detail level simpler than the cast.

Priority:
1. official location anchors;
2. action-supporting structures;
3. paths and circulation;
4. functional props;
5. decoration.

## Magic

Magic is legible through purpose, not random light. Every visible spell effect should answer “what is this person doing?”

## Anti-drift

Reject:
- deep cinematic perspective;
- tiny rear crowds;
- generic fantasy market/camp substitutions;
- meaningless glowing effects;
- race/species anatomy drift;
- all characters wearing unrelated costumes;
- background complexity exceeding character complexity.
