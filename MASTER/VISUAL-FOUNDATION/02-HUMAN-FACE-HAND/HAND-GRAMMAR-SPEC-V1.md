# VA02-HAND-GRAMMAR — Humanoid Hand Grammar Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Parent style:** VA01 — Style Calibration V1

## Purpose
Lock one hand-construction and gesture system for humanoid assets.

## Controls
- palm/finger proportion;
- knuckle simplification;
- line weight;
- glove vs bare-hand rendering;
- grip logic;
- readable gesture silhouette.

## Required future board
At least 8 isolated hands: open, point, fist, grip weapon, hold book, pass object, write, carry.

## Non-controls
Do not inherit exact prop, sleeve, glove, species anatomy, role or faction.

## Lock prerequisite
Do not generate/lock this board until VA02-FACE-GRAMMAR is approved, because Face Grammar is the current blocking visual inconsistency.
