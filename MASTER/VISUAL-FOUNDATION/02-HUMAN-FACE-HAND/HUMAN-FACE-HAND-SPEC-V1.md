# VA02 — Human / Face / Hand Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Seed authority:** SEED-A primary. SEED-E controls board organization only.

## Controls
VA02 locks the project’s human baseline:
- adult body proportion;
- face simplification/detail;
- age variation;
- body-type variation;
- hand construction and gesture readability;
- readable facial acting at Hidden-Object scale.

It does not create reusable named actors and does not define nonhuman species anatomy.

## Body proportion
- Adult baseline: approximately **5–5.5 heads tall**.
- Compact and graphic, but unmistakably adult.
- Legs and arms remain anatomically functional; do not shorten limbs into chibi construction.
- Heads may be slightly larger than realistic illustration for readability, but not “cute oversized.”
- Body type may vary: lean, average, broad, heavy, older/softer, athletic.

## Face grammar
- Eyes readable but not anime-large.
- Nose and mouth simplified with stable line construction.
- Facial planes indicated through restrained cel shading rather than realistic skin rendering.
- Adult age should remain visible through shape, hair, posture and limited line detail, not heavy photoreal wrinkles.
- Do not make every face symmetrical or “hero handsome.”

## Hand grammar
Hands must remain readable in:
- open palm;
- pointing;
- gripping weapon/tool;
- holding book/cup/map;
- passing an object;
- writing;
- carrying.

Finger count and grip logic matter more than anatomical micro-detail.

## Required board content
- 6–8 full-body adult humans sharing one ground line.
- At least three age bands among adults.
- At least four body types.
- 6 face insets showing consistent style under different ages/expressions.
- 8 hand/gesture insets.
- At least two figures holding small readable props.
- White or very light neutral background.

## Board-layout inheritance from SEED-E
Inherit shared ground line, full-body clarity, spacing, comparable scale and equipment visibility.

Do not inherit SEED-E’s literal species roster or anatomy.

## Pass criteria
- All humans visibly belong to one illustration system.
- Variation does not create different cartoon styles.
- No childlike adult anatomy.
- Hands support actions rather than hiding behind props.
- Faces remain readable at asset-board review scale.
- No named recurring cast is created.

## Reject conditions
- anime eyes;
- photoreal portrait faces;
- 7.5–8-head realistic fashion anatomy;
- 3–4-head chibi anatomy;
- mitten hands;
- repeated identical face template.
