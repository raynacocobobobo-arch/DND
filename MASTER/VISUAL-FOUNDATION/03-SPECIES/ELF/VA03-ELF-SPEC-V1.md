# VA03-ELF — Elf Species Variation Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Parent style:** VA01  
**Humanoid grammar:** VA02

## Controls
Locks Elf anatomy and variation space: near-human adult height, lighter/narrower skeletal read, adult facial grammar and pointed ears.

## Required board
Six adult Elves, one ground line, white/light background. Include short/average/tall within Elf range; lean/average/broader builds; young-adult/adult/older adult; male/female presentation.

## Invariants
- near Human height, roughly 100–103% project baseline;
- pointed ears;
- adult, not childlike;
- generally lighter/narrower structural read than Human baseline without forcing every Elf to be extremely thin.

## Variables
Height within species band, body type, age, skin tone, face family, hair, ear-detail variation within restrained limits.

## Do not inherit
Clothing, faction, profession, hairstyle, color palette.

## Forbidden misreads
Anime elf ears, childlike body, every Elf identical and thin, extreme giant height, species signaled only by green clothing.
