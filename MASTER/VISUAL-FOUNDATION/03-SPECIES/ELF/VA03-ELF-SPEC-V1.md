# VA03-ELF — Elf Species Variation Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Parent style:** VA01 — Style Calibration V1  
**Face grammar:** VA02-FACE-GRAMMAR — current positive diagnostic, not yet LOCKED  
**Research authority:** `MASTER/DND5E-MASTER-RESEARCH-AND-15-SCENES-V1.md` + approved S02 Species Scale

## Purpose

Lock a reusable Elf anatomy system for later character sheets and 55–70-character Hidden-Object scenes.

The repository's existing Elf definition is the authority:

- adult height near Human scale;
- approximately **100–103% Human baseline** in project production;
- overall **longer visual line**;
- **lighter / narrower mass** than Human baseline;
- pointed ears;
- adult, slightly finer facial structure;
- elegant but not fragile;
- 2024 project lineage coverage: **Drow / High Elf / Wood Elf**.

The Elf board must not become “Human bodies with pointed ears.”

## Species construction

### Shared Elf skeleton

All lineages share the same Elf species skeleton.

Primary visual differences from Human:
- narrower shoulder/torso mass;
- lighter limb mass;
- cleaner, longer silhouette;
- slightly finer adult facial structure;
- pointed ear anatomy;
- posture may feel lighter / more economical, but not theatrical or ethereal.

Do not make Elves substantially taller than Human.
Do not make them child-thin.
Do not use head-size changes to create elegance.

## Body Chassis

Elf variation is narrower than Human variation.

VA03-ELF V1 uses **three**, not four, chassis:

### E-B01 — SLENDER LIGHT
- narrow shoulders and torso;
- light limbs;
- adult skeleton;
- long visual line;
- not frail, adolescent or chibi.

### E-B02 — STANDARD ELVEN
- baseline Elf frame;
- moderate but still light mass;
- visibly narrower/lighter than Human B02;
- primary reusable Elf chassis.

### E-B03 — ATHLETIC
- visibly trained / stronger frame;
- defined shoulders, back, arms and legs;
- athletic rather than bulky;
- must remain narrower and lighter than Human B03.

**There is no Elf equivalent of Human B04 BROAD / HEAVY in this V1 mother system.**

Some individual Elves may be fuller or broader than others, but that remains an identity-level variation inside E-B02/E-B03, not a separate heavy chassis.

## Height / body ratio

Project production:
- ordinary Elf stature: approximately **1.00–1.03 Human baseline**;
- head/body relation remains inside the project's existing adult **~5–5.5-head** stylization band;
- do not invent a separate tall-Elf body ratio.

The longer Elf read must come mainly from:
- narrower mass;
- cleaner silhouette;
- posture;
- subtle proportional distribution;

not from making the head smaller or the body dramatically taller.

## Elf Lineages

The next board must explicitly cover all three 2024 project lineages.

### High Elf
Repository visual guidance:
- lineage may later read through scholarly / magical behavior;
- do not encode High Elf as “everyone wears ornate robes.”

On the anatomy board:
- keep costume neutral;
- use lineage label;
- preserve common Elf skeleton.

### Wood Elf
Repository visual guidance:
- lineage may later read through outdoor equipment, movement and environmental relationship;
- do not encode Wood Elf as “green clothing + bow.”

On the anatomy board:
- keep costume neutral;
- use lineage label;
- preserve common Elf skeleton.

### Drow
Repository visual guidance:
- Drow remains the same Elf skeleton and must not become a separate species.

On the anatomy board:
- include Drow identities explicitly;
- preserve the same body-chassis and Face Grammar system;
- do not exaggerate into a demonic or alien anatomy system.

Any lineage-specific skin/hair palette treatment should follow verified official visual references rather than generic fantasy invention.

## Ear Construction Core

All six samples use one restrained Elf ear system:
- clearly pointed;
- moderate length;
- integrated into an adult skull;
- same basic root / taper grammar across all samples.

Allowed:
- small variation in angle;
- small variation in taper;
- partial exposure caused by hair.

Forbidden:
- extremely long horizontal anime ears;
- oversized fairy ears;
- a second ear construction system for Drow;
- using giant ears as the main species identifier.

At least one ear must be readable per sample.

## Face Grammar

Faces inherit the current positive VA02 Face Grammar diagnostic.

Elf identity may use somewhat finer / narrower face shapes than Human, but must preserve:
- one eye-size/detail family;
- one iris/pupil simplification;
- one nose simplification level;
- one mouth/teeth system;
- one line hierarchy;
- one restrained cel-shading system.

No “anime Elf face.”

## Hair rule

Hair is an **identity variable**, not an Elf species marker.

The earlier requirement to maximize hair-family diversity is retired because it caused over-styled modern fantasy hair to dominate the board.

Use subdued, practical hair families such as:
- straight shoulder-length;
- straight or softly wavy long hair;
- simple low braid;
- simple low ponytail / half-tie;
- short swept hair;
- restrained older hair.

Avoid:
- giant high buns;
- salon-style curled updos;
- excessive ringlets;
- exaggerated messy fantasy volume;
- hair designs whose main purpose is to expose the ears.

Hair should allow at least one ear to remain readable without becoming a visual gimmick.

## Shared board construction

All six figures:
- one exact shared ground line;
- white/light neutral background;
- orthographic-like front/slight 3/4 standing presentation;
- same adult project stylization band;
- same relaxed stance family;
- both hands readable;
- simple fitted neutral fantasy clothing;
- no faction-specific uniform;
- no armor / long cloaks / giant props hiding body;
- at least one ear readable;
- one Face Grammar;
- VA01 line / flat color / restrained cel shading.

## Clothing rule

Species must read through anatomy, not costume.

Use a restrained common clothing family:
- simple fitted tunic/shirt;
- narrow belt;
- trousers;
- low/mid boots.

Small neutral variations are allowed.

Do not use:
- green as Wood Elf shorthand;
- ornate robes as High Elf shorthand;
- black leather as Drow shorthand;
- bows as Elf shorthand.

Lineage labels may appear beneath figures for this diagnostic board.

## Pass criteria

The board passes only if:
1. every figure reads as adult Elf before reading the lineage label;
2. all six share one Elf species skeleton;
3. Drow / High / Wood are all represented;
4. Drow does not become a separate species;
5. E-B01 / B02 / B03 are distinguishable but remain inside the light/narrow Elf envelope;
6. no figure reaches Human B04 broad/heavy mass;
7. E-B03 remains athletic rather than Human-bulky;
8. pointed ears use one restrained construction system;
9. hair remains subordinate to anatomy;
10. Face Grammar remains consistent;
11. no body difference is created by head-size changes or perspective.

## Reject conditions

Reject if:
- the board is six generic Elves with no lineage coverage;
- any sample is primarily “Human + pointed ears”;
- any Elf becomes broad/heavy like Human B04;
- one lineage uses a different skeleton;
- Drow becomes demonic/alien;
- ears become anime/fairy caricatures;
- hair becomes high-volume salon/fantasy styling;
- lineage is communicated only by costume color stereotypes;
- head/body ratio changes to create elegance;
- Face Grammar drift reappears.
