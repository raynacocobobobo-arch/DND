# VA03-ELF — Elf Species Variation Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Parent style:** VA01 — Style Calibration V1  
**Face grammar:** VA02-FACE-GRAMMAR — current positive diagnostic, not yet LOCKED

## Purpose

Lock a reusable Elf anatomy system for later character sheets and 55–70-character Hidden-Object scenes.

VA03-ELF follows the same production principle proven useful for Human:

> **finite species-specific Body Chassis + narrow stature variation + identity variables**

The goal is not to create one canonical Elf mannequin and not to create an unrestricted height/body slider space.

## Species relationship to Human

Project production controls:
- Elf adult stature remains near Human scale;
- ordinary Elf stature band: approximately **1.00–1.03 Human baseline**;
- Elf construction uses a slightly longer/lighter visual line than Human while remaining fully adult;
- all Elf samples use one fixed project body ratio of approximately **5.4 heads**;
- pointed ears are mandatory but restrained.

These are project visual-production controls, not universal D&D canonical measurements.

## Elf Body Chassis

VA03-ELF V1 uses exactly four base chassis.

### E-B01 — SLENDER LIGHT

Structural read:
- narrow shoulders and torso;
- light limb mass;
- long clean visual line;
- adult skeleton, never childlike or fragile-chibi.

### E-B02 — STANDARD ELVEN

Structural read:
- baseline Elf frame;
- moderate shoulder/torso/limb mass;
- clearly lighter/narrower than Human B02 without becoming extremely thin.

### E-B03 — ATHLETIC / STRONG

Structural read:
- visibly stronger shoulders, back, arms and legs;
- athletic rather than bulky;
- remains recognizably Elf and does not become Human B03 with pointed ears.

Sex presentation does not determine access to this chassis.

### E-B04 — BROAD / SOFT

Structural read:
- broader torso/pelvis and fuller soft-tissue volume relative to other Elves;
- still narrower/lighter in overall species read than Human B04;
- adult, stable skeleton;
- not a caricature.

## Stature rule

Elf stature is secondary to chassis and identity.

Allowed project band:
- minimum ordinary Elf sample: `1.00` Human baseline;
- maximum ordinary Elf sample: `1.03` Human baseline.

Do not create SHORT / AVG / TALL Elf mother bodies.

Do not change head/body ratio, limb construction or head size to create stature variation.

## Ear Construction Core

All six samples use one Elf ear system:
- clearly pointed;
- moderate length;
- integrated into a normal adult cranial structure;
- no extreme horizontal anime ears;
- no oversized fairy ears;
- no sample may use a second ear drawing system.

Allowed ear variation:
- small changes in angle;
- small changes in taper;
- small changes in exposed length due to hair.

At least one ear must remain readable on every sample so the board can validate species anatomy.

## Face Grammar

Faces inherit the current positive VA02 Face Grammar diagnostic:
- one eye-size/detail family;
- one iris/pupil simplification;
- one nose simplification level;
- one mouth/teeth system;
- one line hierarchy;
- one restrained cel-shading system.

Elf identity may have somewhat finer/narrower facial shapes than Human, but may not switch into anime-elf face grammar.

## E01–E06 generation test contract

Use six adult Elf identities on one exact shared ground line.

| ID | Chassis | Age | Sex presentation | Stature | Primary test |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| E01 | E-B01 SLENDER LIGHT | adult | masculine | 1.00 | light adult Elf without childlike read |
| E02 | E-B02 STANDARD ELVEN | adult | feminine | 1.01 | baseline Elf |
| E03 | E-B03 ATHLETIC / STRONG | adult | feminine | 1.02 | strong female Elf without Human-body drift |
| E04 | E-B03 ATHLETIC / STRONG | adult | masculine | 1.03 | same chassis, different identity/sex presentation |
| E05 | E-B04 BROAD / SOFT | adult | masculine | 1.01 | broad/soft Elf without caricature |
| E06 | E-B02 STANDARD ELVEN | older adult | feminine | 1.01 | age transfer on baseline chassis |

Critical pair tests:
- E03 ↔ E04: same E-B03 chassis, different identities / sex presentation;
- E02 ↔ E06: same E-B02 chassis, different age / identity.

## Shared board construction

All six figures:
- white/light neutral background;
- exact shared ground line;
- orthographic-like front/slight 3/4 standing presentation;
- approximately 5.4 heads;
- same relaxed standing stance family;
- both hands visible;
- simple fitted fantasy calibration clothing;
- no faction uniform;
- no armor or oversized robes hiding anatomy;
- at least one pointed ear visible;
- one Face Grammar;
- VA01 line / flat color / restrained cel shading.

## Clothing control

Use one shared simple clothing family:
- fitted tunic/shirt;
- narrow belt;
- trousers;
- low/mid boots.

Small neckline, sleeve and color changes are allowed.

Do not use:
- green clothing as Elf shorthand;
- long cloaks hiding torso width;
- oversized shoulder pieces;
- large props;
- elaborate ranger/wizard stereotypes.

Species must read through anatomy, not costume.

## Identity-distribution controls

Across E01–E06:
- six different facial identities;
- at least three skin-tone families;
- at least four hair families;
- adult age variation;
- sex presentation independent from chassis;
- no duplicate “blond archer Elf” templates;
- hair must not hide both ears.

## Invariants

- unmistakably adult Elf;
- pointed ears using one restrained ear grammar;
- near-Human stature inside 1.00–1.03 production band;
- approximately 5.4 heads throughout;
- lighter/narrower species read than Human baseline;
- one of four approved Elf chassis;
- one Face Grammar;
- readable hands;
- no perspective height cheat.

## Reject conditions

Reject if:
- any Elf becomes childlike;
- ear size/shape jumps into anime/fairy extremes;
- every Elf is the same thin mannequin;
- E03/E04 become Human B03 bodies with pointed ears;
- E05 becomes Human B04 with pointed ears;
- sex presentation determines chassis;
- stature becomes a visible SHORT / AVG / TALL class system;
- head/body ratio changes between samples;
- clothing, green palette or bows are doing the species identification;
- older age switches to a realistic face-rendering system;
- Face Grammar drift reappears.

## On-image labels

Only:
- `E01`–`E06`;
- chassis tag: `EB01 / EB02 / EB03 / EB04`;
- optional `OLDER` for E06;
- one shared `~5.4 HEADS` note.

No long explanatory text on the board.
