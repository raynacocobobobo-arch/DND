# VA03-HALFLING — Halfling Species Variation Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Parent style:** VA01  
**Humanoid grammar:** VA02

## Controls
Locks Halfling adult anatomy and variation space.

## Required board
Six adult Halflings with short/average/tall-within-species and lean/average/broad/heavy variation, multiple ages and male/female presentation.

## Invariants
- roughly 56–60% Human production height;
- compact small-adult skeleton;
- clearly adult face/posture;
- readable hands/feet at board scale.

## Variables
Body mass, face, age, hair, skin tone, neutral clothing.

## Do not inherit
Bare feet, rustic clothing, cheerful personality, profession.

## Forbidden misreads
Toddler/child proportions, simply shrinking a Human asset, oversized head for cuteness, species identity dependent on costume.
