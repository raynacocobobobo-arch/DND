# VA03-DWARF — Dwarf Species Variation Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Parent style:** VA01  
**Humanoid grammar:** VA02

## Controls
Locks Dwarf adult anatomy and variation space.

## Required board
Six adult Dwarves, shared ground line, white/light background. Include leaner, average, broad/strong, heavier and older examples; both male/female presentation.

## Invariants
- adult height around 80–83% Human production baseline;
- dense torso and broad skeletal read;
- relatively shorter powerful limbs;
- substantial hands/forearms;
- unmistakably adult face/posture.

## Variables
Height within band, body mass, beard/no beard where appropriate, age, face, hair, skin tone, neutral clothing.

## Do not inherit
Occupation, mining stereotype, beard as mandatory species marker, faction clothes.

## Forbidden misreads
Scaled-down Human, child body, every Dwarf obese, every Dwarf male/bearded, perspective-created height difference.
