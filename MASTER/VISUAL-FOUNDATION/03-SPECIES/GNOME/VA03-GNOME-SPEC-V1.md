# VA03-GNOME — Gnome Species Variation Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Parent style:** VA01  
**Humanoid grammar:** VA02

## Controls
Locks Gnome adult anatomy and variation space, distinct from Halfling.

## Required board
Six adult Gnomes with height/body/age/sex-presentation variation on one ground line.

## Invariants
- roughly 60–64% Human production height;
- small/light adult build, generally narrower structural read than Dwarf;
- clearly distinct adult face/silhouette from Halfling.

## Variables
Body type, age, face family, hair, skin tone, neutral clothing.

## Do not inherit
Pointed hats, gadget profession, comic personality, faction clothing.

## Forbidden misreads
Child wizard, Halfling clone with pointed hat, Dwarf clone without beard, oversize cute head.
