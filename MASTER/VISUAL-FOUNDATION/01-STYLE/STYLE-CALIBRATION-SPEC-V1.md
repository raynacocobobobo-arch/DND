# VA01 — Style Calibration Spec V1

**Status:** DRAFT SPEC  
**Seed authority:** SEED-A primary; SEED-D secondary for ensemble readability only. SEED-E is not a rendering-style authority.

## Controls
VA01 locks the general rendering grammar shared by all 15 levels:
- black hand-drawn contour language;
- line hierarchy;
- flat-color behavior;
- restrained cel shading;
- highlight restraint;
- saturation discipline;
- edge clarity;
- character/background detail hierarchy.

It does **not** lock species anatomy, faction clothing, location architecture, scene lighting, or UI.

## Visual target
The project should read as a clean European fantasy adventure ensemble comic: graphic, hand-drawn and highly readable, with enough material detail for Hidden-Object play but without painterly or cinematic rendering.

### Line hierarchy
- Character outer contour: strongest line family.
- Internal clothing/anatomy seams: medium line family.
- Small prop/material detail: lighter line family.
- Background architecture: one perceptual detail/line level below characters.
- Avoid equal-strength black outlining on every background crack, brick or leaf.

### Color
- Clear local colors.
- Bright but controlled saturation.
- Adjacent characters should separate through value/hue as well as outline.
- Skin, cloth, leather, metal and stone must remain distinguishable without photoreal texture.
- No global muddy brown wash.
- No neon-everywhere fantasy grading.

### Shadow / light
- One restrained cel-shadow family per form is the default.
- Secondary occlusion may be used where necessary for readability.
- Highlights are selective, especially on metal/glass/magic.
- No airbrushed realism, bloom haze or cinematic volumetric-light dependency.

## Required board panels
The VA01 board must visibly demonstrate:
1. one full-body human-scale adventurer;
2. one face close-up;
3. one readable hand + carried object;
4. two-person interaction;
5. four-to-six-person micro-event;
6. cloth / leather / metal comparison;
7. stone / wood / parchment comparison;
8. a small architecture/background sample showing reduced detail;
9. one “same style at smaller screen scale” crop.

No panel may contain UI.

## Seed inheritance
### From SEED-A
Inherit character clarity, compact adult fantasy proportions, clean black line, flat/cel rendering, readable hands/faces and natural gestures.

### From SEED-D
Inherit only the principle that many local events can coexist without losing legibility.

### Explicitly excluded
B and C are not positive references and must not influence VA01.

## Pass criteria
- One coherent line family across every panel.
- Character detail clearly outranks background detail.
- No painterly brush texture.
- No 3D-render material response.
- No anime/manga facial system.
- No chibi body construction.
- No depth-of-field blur.
- No photoreal skin.
- At 25–35% board scale, character silhouettes and actions remain readable.

## Reject conditions
Reject the entire board if one or more panels drift into a different art family. A “mostly correct” board with one painterly, 3D or anime panel is not lockable.
