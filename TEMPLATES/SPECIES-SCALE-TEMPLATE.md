## Cross-species staging rules
- Foreground figure scale target: 105–110%.
- Core midground: 100%.
- Rear/platform: 85–90%.
- These are **screen composition scales**, not lore height claims.
- Small species must remain adult, not child-proportioned.
- Large species must preserve mass and limb proportions, not become enlarged humans.

## Failure examples to reject
- Dwarf/gnome rendered as children.
- Dragonborn anatomy reduced to human + cosmetic head.
- Tail/horn/ear anatomy drifting between characters.
- Rear figures shrinking beyond readability.
