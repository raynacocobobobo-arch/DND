## Scene being populated
**Court of Air / visitor interface**, around 10:30 on a fair, coastal-windy morning. New Seekers are being registered and routed while normal Avowed work continues and an Endless Chant procession crosses the shared public space.

The model below is a `【PROJECT】` production design grounded in the approved Gate 0–1 research. It is **not** an official Candlekeep population census.

## Population principle
Candlekeep must read first as a **working knowledge institution**. Therefore:
1. institutional residents/staff are the structural base;
2. Seekers and other visitors are present because of the admission/research workflow;
3. species diversity follows role and visitor logic;
4. no unusual species is inserted merely to make the crowd look “more D&D”;
5. the target is 60 readable figures because this subscene can support it, not because every level must equal 60.

### Why zero non-humanoid creatures
The public visitor workflow already provides enough event density. A familiar, magical animal, spectral guardian, or decorative creature would currently be spectacle-first rather than role-first. Miirym is explicitly excluded by Gate 1.

## Readability / crowd-density model
The 60 figures should not form one queue.

Proposed density:
- **Core visitor-processing band:** ~24 figures.
- **Endless Chant moving band:** 10 figures.
- **Secondary institutional traffic:** ~14 figures.
- **Visitor waiting / repacking / discussion pockets:** ~12 figures.

The later Event-Island stage will turn these into 8–12 interaction groups; this document only proves that the population ecology can support them.
