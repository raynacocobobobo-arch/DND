## Scope
This file proposes the relative body construction for the six species currently proposed in the Candlekeep population model.

If Gate 2 is approved, it locks the **scene-relative species set and scale relationships** for Candlekeep. Cross-project anatomy remains governed by the Global Visual Foundation Species Master before Gate 3, so this file must not become an independent competing anatomy bible.

The percentages below are `【PROJECT】` production ratios anchored to a Human adult baseline of 100%. They are **not official population statistics or universal canonical heights**. Individual adults can vary inside a narrow band without breaking the species silhouette.

## Human baseline
- Screen/lore construction baseline: **100%**
- Adult anatomy: project stylization baseline from `MASTER/STYLE-LOCK-V1.md`, approximately **5–5.5 heads tall**
- Use: reference for relative body height and mass
- Do not standardize every Human to the same build.

## Species-specific construction notes

### Elf
- Keep body height around Human baseline.
- Differentiate through proportion, posture, ear anatomy, and finer build—not by making every Elf extremely tall or ethereal.
- Institutional Elves still wear the Avowed visual language if they are Avowed.

### Dwarf
- Shorter legs and compact torso are structural, not merely scaled-down Human anatomy.
- Shoulders/forearms/hands can carry more visual mass.
- Adult face and mature posture are mandatory.
- Do not inflate head size to “cute” proportions.

### Halfling
- Much shorter than Human baseline.
- Keep adult hands, face, posture, and clothing construction.
- Readability is protected by blocking and clean silhouette, **not** by making the character too tall.

### Gnome
- Similar small-category screen challenge to Halfling but should remain distinguishable through body build, face, hair, costume details, and role.
- Do not use exaggerated pointed hats as species shorthand.

### Tiefling
- Horn geometry must be planned per character and remain consistent across front/3-4 views.
- Tail must be staged so it does not create false overlaps with nearby figures.
- No wings unless a specifically sourced character concept requires them; none are planned for this Gate 2 cast.

## Camera/readability interaction
The global blocking screen-scale bands remain separate from species anatomy:
- foreground placement: 105–110% screen treatment;
- core midground: 100%;
- rear/platform: 85–90%.

A rear Dwarf is still a Dwarf relative to nearby Humans; the whole rear band may be screen-scaled together for hidden-object readability.

## Pairwise calibration anchors
Use these checks on character sheets:
1. Human + Elf: similar overall height, different build/silhouette.
2. Human + Dwarf: Dwarf clearly shorter and substantially broader.
3. Human + Halfling: Halfling around three-fifths Human height, unmistakably adult.
4. Halfling + Gnome: similar small category but not identical skeleton/face.
5. Human + Tiefling: similar body scale; species read through horns/tail/anatomy, not height gimmick.
