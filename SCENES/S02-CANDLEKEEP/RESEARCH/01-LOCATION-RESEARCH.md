## Evidence policy

## Location identity
- Candlekeep is a fortress-like institution devoted to collecting and preserving written knowledge. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`
- It stands on a rocky coastal height by the Sea of Swords and is visually defined by massive defensive walls and many towers/spires. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`
- The location must read first as an old, defended knowledge institution—not as a generic wizard school or magical marketplace. `【PROJECT】`

## Spatial hierarchy relevant to this level
Official material distinguishes the entry/gate sequence, the **Court of Air**, the **Inner Ward**, the Great Library, Exaltation, and other spires/cloisters. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`

For this project, the playable slice is the public-facing visitor interface associated with the **Court of Air**, before any attempt to depict restricted deeper spaces. `【PROJECT】`

### Unresolved spatial details
The following must remain open until the poster map and accessible body text are checked at blocking time:
- exact building orientation around the Court of Air;
- exact public-facing sightlines from entry spaces;
- precise relationship among entrance spaces, the Emerald Door, and nearby service structures;
- which architectural elements can be simultaneously visible from the chosen camera.

These unknowns are **not** permission to invent a convenient fantasy courtyard. `【PROJECT】`

## Admission custom
Visitors are called **Seekers**. To enter Candlekeep, a visitor presents a written work that the collection does not already possess and that is judged to have value. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`

This admission custom creates an authentic visible workflow for the level:
- Seeker arrives with written material;
- an Avowed-facing process evaluates/accepts/routes the visitor;
- records, manuscripts, cases, ledgers, ink, parchment, seals, and carrying containers can appear as functional props. `【PROJECT】`

The exact desk placement, queue geometry, and number of officials are production decisions, not official lore. `【PROJECT】`

## The Avowed
Candlekeep is maintained by **the Avowed**, an institutional community organized around scholarship, preservation, and Candlekeep’s rules. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`

Official public material identifies ranks/roles including:
- Keeper of Tomes;
- First Reader;
- Great Readers;
- Master Readers;
- Chanter;
- Gatewarden;
- Avowed Adjutants. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`

For this level, rank and **duty** should drive visual differentiation more strongly than fantasy-costume randomness. `【PROJECT】`

## Endless Chant
The **Endless Chant**, associated with the Chanter and the recitation of Alaundo’s prophecies, is a repeated institutional behavior strongly tied to Candlekeep’s identity. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`

The level may use a passing chant procession as a secondary event because it communicates “this is Candlekeep” through behavior rather than decorative lore symbols. `【PROJECT】`

## Population logic
### Permanent residents / institutional staff
Plausible visible functions include Avowed readers/sages, scribes, gate-facing staff, guides/adjutants, manuscript handlers, and other knowledge-work roles. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】` / `【PROJECT】`

### Temporary visitors
Seekers may include scholars, adventurers, clerics, wizards, couriers, and other people whose reason for presence is tied to seeking knowledge or delivering written material. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】` / `【PROJECT】`

### Species
Candlekeep must **not** be treated as a human-only institution. Official material supports nonhuman members/visitors, but no authoritative public species percentage is being claimed here. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`

Therefore:
- do not create an “11 species evenly distributed” crowd;
- species diversity follows role, origin, visitor logic, and available evidence;
- institutional identity should visually bind the Avowed more strongly than species variety separates them. `【PROJECT】`

## SRD production mapping
Useful shared substrate includes:
- Mage / Archmage / Acolyte / Priest archetype logic where role-appropriate;
- books, parchment, ink, spell scrolls, magnifying tools;
- calligrapher / scholarly tool behavior;
- research-facing spells such as Detect Magic, Identify, and Comprehend Languages when they have a concrete task. `【SRD-5.1】` / `【SRD-5.2.1】`

A spell effect is never included only to make the scene “look magical.” `【PROJECT】`

## Props and material language
Supported/appropriate production anchors include manuscripts, scroll cases, ink, quills, wax/seals, ledgers, satchels, book/manuscript transport containers, copying surfaces, old clean masonry, arches, towers, paper and parchment. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】` / `【PROJECT】`

Exact costume details for Avowed must remain tied to verified official visual references. Generic wizard robes are not an acceptable substitute. `【PROJECT】`

## Creatures / supernatural elements
Miirym is associated with Candlekeep’s deeper/subterranean lore, but she is **not** part of this public visitor-interface scene. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】` / `【PROJECT】`

Do not place a spectral dragon in the courtyard simply to advertise Candlekeep lore. A later decorative reference would require its own source and must not compete with the visitor workflow. `【PROJECT】`
