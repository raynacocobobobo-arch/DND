## Selected subscene
**Court of Air / visitor interface**. `【PROJECT】`

Production boundary:
> The public-facing Candlekeep arrival/visitor-processing space associated with the Court of Air, after arrival at the fortress and before any attempt to depict restricted Inner Ward knowledge spaces.

## Why this slice wins
1. The location’s defining **admission custom** becomes visible action instead of exposition. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】` / `【PROJECT】`
2. Seekers and Avowed can plausibly coexist in one readable ensemble without inventing a festival. `【OFFICIAL-SETTING】`
3. Written material, registers, routing, manuscript handling, guides, and the Endless Chant can create multiple event islands while remaining ordinary Candlekeep activity. `【PROJECT】`
4. The image can communicate “fortified knowledge institution” without needing the deepest or most spectacular spaces. `【PROJECT】`
5. It is distinct from the market, dock, festival, battlefield, court, and carnival scenes elsewhere in the 15-level set. `【PROJECT】`

## Explicit exclusions
### Great Library interior
Excluded from this level. It is iconic but shifts the scene away from the visitor-interface behavior selected for a large ensemble. `【PROJECT】`

### Exaltation
Excluded from this level. Do not collapse multiple named Candlekeep interiors into a single “best of Candlekeep” illustration. `【PROJECT】`

### Miirym / subterranean area
Excluded from this level. The hidden/deeper guardian lore is not part of the public Court of Air scene. `【PROJECT】`

### Unrelated Candlekeep Mysteries incidents
Excluded. No adventure-specific outbreak, combat, cursed-book crisis, or mystery climax is imported merely to add spectacle. `【PROJECT】`

## Spatial caution
